Computer Vision

What is Computer Vision

Computer Vision is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that allows machines to process, analyze and interpret visual information, like images and audio. This uses machine learning in order to help computers and other systems get useful information from visual data.

Effect on Minority

Computer Vision allows for people with visual impairments to increase their independence and autonomy, it can do this by describing senses, recognizing objects, and recognizing faces.
