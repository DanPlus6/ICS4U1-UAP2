Brain-Computer Interfaces

What are Brain-Computer Interfaces?

A Brain-Computer Interface is a technology that makes direct communication between the brain and external devices possible. It interprets brain signals to control computers or machines, potentially helping people with disabilities to be able to communicate, operate assistive devices, and interact with technology mentally.

Brain-computer interfaces have the potential to transform Canadian society by making direct communication between brains and devices, which improves accessibility and efficiency of digital technology usage. While this could transform how we interact with digital technology in the future, it raises privacy and ethical concerns.
