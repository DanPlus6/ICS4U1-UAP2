Post-Quantum Cryptography

What is Post-Quantum Cryptography?

Post-quantum cryptography is a cybersecurity technology created to protect sensitive information from future quantum computers that could break current encryption. It uses mathematical concepts, like structured lattices and has functions which are difficult for quantum computers to solve, helping secure information safely.

This technology helps protect global financial systems from quantum cyberattacks, helping secure important economic information and transactions. This prevents disruptions that could affect international markets and businesses.
